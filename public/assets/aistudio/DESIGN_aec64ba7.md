## Brand & Style

This design system translates the legendary hospitality, aristocratic heritage, and lavish courts of the Nizams of Hyderabad into an opulent, immersive digital destination. Designed for discerning patrons seeking elevated culinary heritage, the aesthetic evokes midnight feasts (*mehfils*), slow-simmered aromatic dum preparations, velvet drapes, and chased brass vessels.

The visual direction unites **Heritage Luxury** with **Sensory High-Contrast Darkness**. Drawing inspiration from Mughal-Deccani architectural grandeur, the interface pairs deep charcoal-vignetted canvas depths and rich velvet burgundy with imperial emerald accents and luminous antique gold filigree. Delicate antique gold diamond glyphs (❖), hairline metallic borders, and jewel-tone pill badges create an unmistakably regal, editorial presence that feels historic yet meticulously modern.

## Colors

The color system is rooted in high-contrast Deccani regal palettes, optimized natively for dark mode:

- **Primary (`#4A0A17`)**: Velvet Burgundy. Anchors primary interactive components, highlighted panels, and luxury card backdrops. Extended with darker shades (`#3A000D` and `#260009`) for recessed containers and peripheral vignette fades.
- **Secondary (`#C9A24B`)**: Antique Gold. Provides hair-thin framing, glowing typographic highlights, ornamental diamond glyphs, and iconography. Scaled to `#D4AF37` and luminous `#E5C368` for interactive glow and focal emphasis.
- **Tertiary (`#064E3B`)**: Imperial Jewel Emerald. Deployed purposefully for signature indicators, live availability, bespoke tasting tags, and prestige callouts, with `#047857` used for hovered jade surfaces.
- **Neutral (`#0F0608`)**: Deep Charcoal-Black Vignette. The atmospheric night canvas, ensuring total immersion and velvet depth without clinical grayscale flats.
- **Typography & Ink**: Warm Ivory (`#FFF8F0` and `#FDF4E3`) supplies effortless contrast against deep burgundy and charcoal backdrops, banishing stark white in favor of soft candlelight warmth.

## Typography

The typography stages an evocative dialogue between aristocratic grandeur and modern digital precision:

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Evokes antique Nizami royal decrees and bespoke menu folios. Characterized by high editorial contrast, graceful serifs, and proud verticality. Large titles feature tight tracking, rendered primarily in warm ivory or luminous antique gold.
- **Body & Structural Copy (`Manrope`)**: Provides pristine, modern legibility across dense dish ingredients, tasting sequences, wine pairings, and reservation calendars.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Set in uppercase `Manrope` with intentional letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.12em`). Paired frequently with antique gold glyphs (❖) to delineate courses, spice signatures, and cellar vintages like royal seal marks.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces an unhurried, ceremonial editorial rhythm:

- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: Max container width of 1320px with generous `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Asymmetrical splits (such as 7:5 proportions) allow slow-poured culinary storytelling to accompany stately reservation modules.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Margins compress to `2rem` with `1.25rem` gutters. Staggered card sequences realign into paired luxury columns.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: `1.25rem` margins guarantee generous touch safety. Courses and platters stack linearly with `1rem` to `1.5rem` gaps.
- **Rhythm**: Component spacing strictly adheres to an 8px architectural cadence (`0.25rem` to `2.5rem`), allowing elements breathing room reminiscent of spacious palace courtyards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects clinical dropped shadows in favor of **Tonal Velvet Layering**, **Hairline Antique Gold Framing**, and **Subtle Warm Ambient Glows**:

- **Level 0 (Atmospheric Canvas)**: Deep charcoal-black vignette (`#0F0608`) combined with soft peripheral radial gradients bleeding into deep velvet burgundy (`#260009`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Containers)**: Subtle elevation achieved via rich velvet burgundy surfaces (`#3A000D` or `#4A0A17`), enclosed in a 1px hairline border of Antique Gold (`#C9A24B`) at 25% opacity.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Overlays & Dockets)**: Elevated velvet surfaces edged in 1px Antique Gold (`#D4AF37` at 45% opacity) accompanied by a deep, warm ambient shadow: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(15, 6, 8, 0.85), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(201, 162, 75, 0.2)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal & Reservation Domes)**: Heavy midnight backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background-color: rgba(15, 6, 8, 0.85)`), bringing royal center-stage focus to booking dialogues framed with delicate gold corners.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft (`1`)** shape language (`0.25rem` base roundedness, `0.5rem` for cards and overlays, `0.75rem` for expansive modular enclosures).

Rounded corners are deliberately subdued to preserve the formal dignity of stone architecture and royal parchment. The sole exception is reserved for **Jewel Pill Tags** and status lozenges, which employ a fully rounded border radius (`9999px`) to create tactile, gem-cut contrasts against rectangular, hairline-framed card architectures.

Delicate ornamental diamond glyphs (`❖`) and subtle cusped arch silhouettes appear at focal boundaries as decorative endcaps and section headers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Royal Gold)**: Background in Antique Gold (`#C9A24B`), text in Deep Charcoal (`#0F0608`), uppercase `Manrope` font with `0.08em` tracking. Hover state transitions to Luminous Gold (`#E5C368`) with a soft gold halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(229, 195, 104, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary (Burgundy Velvet)**: Solid Velvet Burgundy (`#4A0A17`) background, Warm Ivory (`#FFF8F0`) text, and a crisp 1px Antique Gold border (`#C9A24B` at 50% opacity). Hover state shifts background to `#5A0F1F` with 100% border luminosity.
- **Tertiary (Ghost / Heritage Outline)**: Transparent background, 1px solid Antique Gold hairline border, Antique Gold text. Hover fills the container with 10% Antique Gold tint.

### Cards (Banquets, Dum Pukht & Curated Courses)
- Grounded on Deep Velvet Burgundy (`#3A000D` or `#4A0A17`) with 1px hairline Antique Gold borders (`#C9A24B` at 25% opacity).
- Dish titles set in `Playfair Display` Warm Ivory, accompanied by glowing antique gold pricing.
- Signature royal items feature subtle 1px internal gold diamond corner brackets and photography treated with low-contrast warm vignette edges.

### Jewel Pill Tags & Badges
- Pill-shaped lozenges (`9999px` radius) used for curatorial indicators.
- **Imperial Emerald Pill**: Emerald surface (`#064E3B`) with luminous jade typography (`#047857` highlight or Warm Ivory) and hairline gold border for chef signatures like "Dum Pukht" or "Nizami Khas".
- **Antique Gold Pill**: Outlined in Gold (`#C9A24B`) with Warm Ivory text for vintage pairings and dietary credentials.

### Input Fields & Reservation Pickers
- Rich charcoal-burgundy recessed containers (`#260009`) with a 1px Antique Gold boundary (`#C9A24B` at 30% opacity).
- Text entered in Warm Ivory (`#FFF8F0`), placeholder text in muted ivory-gold at 45% opacity.
- Focus state brings a sharp 1px Luminous Gold outline (`#E5C368`) paired with an intimate ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(201, 162, 75, 0.2)`).

### Lists & Degustation Sequences
- Course items spaced with refined horizontal hairline gold dividers tapering to transparent edges.
- Central diamond glyphs (`❖`) separate dish titles from their accompanying regional provenance or year of culinary origin.
- Side notes detailing rare royal spices (mace, saffron, rose extract) rendered in `body-sm` warm ivory with muted opacity.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square (`0.25rem` radius) and circular selectors framed by 1px Antique Gold strokes.
- Checked state fills with solid Imperial Emerald (`#064E3B`) or Velvet Burgundy (`#4A0A17`), displaying an antique gold checkmark or inner gold jewel pip.
