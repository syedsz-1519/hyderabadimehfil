## Brand & Style

This design system translates the legendary hospitality, regal indulgence, and historic grandeur of the Nizams of Hyderabad into a contemporary culinary destination in East London. The aesthetic blends aristocratic warmth with clean modern utility. It evokes sensory richness: simmering saffron, velvet royal courts, aromatic steam rising from slow-cooked clay handis, and ornate architectural jaali screens.

The visual direction pairs **Heritage Luxury** with **Refined Tactility**. Rather than becoming an antique pastiche, the interface grounds traditional motifs—such as cusped arches, fine gold filigree dividers, and geometric lattice work—within modern layout principles, generous negative space, and disciplined typography. The resulting experience feels dignified, warm, appetizing, and culturally rooted.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the royal banquets and architectural stone of the Deccan:

- **Primary (`#5A0F1F`)**: Deep Royal Burgundy. Serves as the anchor for primary navigation, high-emphasis CTAs, and regal surface backdrops.
- **Secondary (`#C9A24B`)**: Antique Gold. Used for delicate framing, active states, key icons, badges, and ornamental separators.
- **Tertiary (`#E8A317`)**: Saffron / Turmeric Gold. An appetizing accent deployed sparingly for highlights, pricing emphasis, live table booking indicators, and taste tags.
- **Neutral (`#1B1411`)**: Charred Charcoal. The definitive ink color for high-contrast legibility against pale surfaces, representing roasted whole spices and night stone.
- **Canvas / Surface Tone**: Warm Cream (`#FBF5EA`) forms the primary light surface, banishing clinical whites in favor of soft, natural parchment warmth.

For dark containers or evening dining modes, reverse the roles: Charred Charcoal forms the deep canvas, Deep Burgundy provides layered depth, and Antique Gold delivers the typographic hierarchy.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between aristocratic cadence and clinical menu readability:

- **Headlines & Titles (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys old-world lineage and bespoke curation. Set with tight tracking in display contexts and generous line-height to maintain graceful proportions.
- **Functional & Body Typography (`Manrope`)**: Ensures modern legibility across dense dish ingredients, allergens, pricing, booking forms, and delivery details.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Rendered in uppercase `Manrope` with wide tracking (`0.05em` to `0.1em`) to mimic stamped royal edicts, gold-inlaid seals, and vintage banquet menus.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces an unhurried, editorial dining cadence:

- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: Max content boundary of 1280px, framed by generous `3rem` canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Asymmetric pairings (e.g., a 7-column story alongside a 5-column sensory banquet frame) introduce bespoke heritage rhythm.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Margin collapses to `2rem`, gutters adjust to `1.25rem`. Two-column food groupings reflow to single stack cards with side imagery.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: `1.25rem` outer margin ensures safe touch zones. Interactive items collapse cleanly into full-width rhythmic stacks with consistent `1rem` vertical intervals.
- **Vertical Hierarchy**: Spacing scales strictly in multiples of 8px (0.5rem base) with `2.5rem` to `4rem` separations between distinct courses or experiential sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids synthetic floating shadows. Instead, it relies on **Tonal Layering**, **Warm Ambient Lighting**, and **Low-Contrast Metallic Trims**:

- **Layer 0 (Base Canvas)**: Warm Cream (`#FBF5EA`) or Deep Charcoal Velvet (`#1B1411`).
- **Layer 1 (Cards & Modules)**: Tonal shifts to pale ivory parchment (`#F5EEDB`) on light mode, or smoked burgundy (`#360812`) on dark mode, bounded by a 1px border of Antique Gold at 20% opacity.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating & Menus)**: Deep diffused shadows tinted with warm pigment: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 20, 17, 0.12)`, paired with a delicate gold hairline stroke (`rgba(201, 162, 75, 0.4)`).
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Overlays)**: Heavy charcoal backdrop wash with subtle blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(27, 20, 17, 0.75)`), drawing focus to the centered vessel modal.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (`1`)** posture (base radius: `0.25rem`, large radius: `0.5rem`). 

Pill shapes and heavy circular geometry are deliberately avoided to preserve architectural discipline. Instead, straight-edged geometries are lightly softened at the corners to resemble hand-pressed royal parchment, cut stone, and chased brass platters. 

For visual punctuation and signature focal points (such as menu section dividers or hero badges), incorporate the silhouette of the **Charminar Arch** (a traditional four-centered Tudor/pointed cusped arch) and delicate geometric **Jaali lattice** motifs as vector mask accents or subtle watermarks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Burgundy (`#5A0F1F`) background, Warm Cream typography (`Manrope`, 600 weight, uppercase tracking), and a 1px inset border of Antique Gold (`#C9A24B`). Subtle hover: lift with background shifting to `#460B18` and gold border brightening.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent fill, 1px solid Antique Gold outline, Charcoal text. Hover state fills the container with 8% Antique Gold wash.
- **Royal Reservation (Hero CTA)**: Saffron Gold (`#E8A317`) background with Charcoal (`#1B1411`) high-contrast text, commanding immediate conversion for table bookings and feast pre-orders.

### Cards (Menu Dishes & Mandi Platters)
- Built on Warm Ivory containers with 1px Antique Gold borders (`rgba(201, 162, 75, 0.25)`).
- Photography is presented with clipped inner bounds or crowned with a soft cusped arch contour.
- Pricing is displayed in Playfair Display serif, highlighted with Saffron Gold.
- Dietary and royal spice indicators (e.g., "Dum Pukht", "Chef’s Signature", "Slow Cooked 8-Hours") sit as micro-badges at top-right.

### Chips & Dietary Filters
- Compact, flat pills with `0.25rem` radius. Inactive: Warm Cream fill with 1px muted bronze outline. Active: Deep Burgundy fill with Warm Cream text and an Antique Gold indicator dot.

### Input Fields & Booking Selectors
- Background tone matched to `#F5EEDB` with a 1px baseline or bounding box in Antique Gold (`#C9A24B` at 40% opacity).
- Focused state transitions the border to Saffron Gold (`#E8A317`) with a faint warm aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px rgba(232, 163, 23, 0.15)`).
- Labels float in uppercase tracking, maintaining an air of bespoke service.

### Lists & Menu Sequences
- Menu listings utilize fine antique gold dotted rules or ornate rosette glyphs separating the dish name and the price.
- Descriptions sit indented beneath, styled in `body-sm` muted charcoal with flavor profile callouts (e.g., cardamom, kewra water, caramelized onions).

### Ornaments & Dividers
- Section dividers feature a central miniature Charminar arch icon or diamond finial flanked by tapering gold hairline rules (`1px` height transitioning to transparent).
